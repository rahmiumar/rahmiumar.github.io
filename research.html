Research, woven from three threads.Riset, ditenun dari tiga benang.

Mathematics education, Rasch measurement, technology-enhanced learning, GeoGebra, teacher education, numeracy, and ethnomathematics. The current focus is on bringing AI into GeoGebra-supported learning trajectories.

Pendidikan matematika, pengukuran Rasch, pembelajaran berbantuan teknologi, GeoGebra, pendidikan guru, numerasi, dan etnomatematika. Fokus saat ini adalah membawa AI ke dalam lintasan belajar berbantuan GeoGebra.

ThemesTema

  1. GeoGebra and AI-supported learningPembelajaran berbantuan GeoGebra dan AI

    Designing and validating learning trajectories that combine GeoGebra applets with AI assistance, such as ChatGPT, to support conceptual understanding of statistical graphs and numeracy.

    Merancang dan memvalidasi lintasan belajar yang memadukan applet GeoGebra dengan bantuan AI, seperti ChatGPT, untuk mendukung pemahaman konseptual grafik statistik dan numerasi.

  2. Rasch and Many-Facet measurementPengukuran Rasch dan Many-Facet

    Applying Rasch Rating Scale and Many-Facet Rasch models to validate instruments, assess bias, and measure change in student reasoning and self-regulated learning.

    Menerapkan model Rasch Rating Scale dan Many-Facet untuk memvalidasi instrumen, menilai bias, dan mengukur perubahan penalaran serta self-regulated learning siswa.

  3. Ethnomathematics and cultureEtnomatematika dan budaya

    Embedding local culture, including traditional food, folklore, and heritage sites, into statistical reasoning and mathematics learning through the Ethno-Flipped Classroom model.

    Menanamkan budaya lokal, termasuk makanan tradisional, cerita rakyat, dan situs warisan, ke dalam penalaran statistik dan pembelajaran matematika melalui model Ethno-Flipped Classroom.
