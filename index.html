Rahmi Ramadhani Umar

Associate Professor of Mathematics Education at Universitas Potensi Utama, Indonesia, and JESH Postdoctoral Fellow of the Austrian Academy of Sciences at Johannes Kepler University Linz. Her research joins GeoGebra and AI-supported learning with ethnomathematics and Rasch measurement.

Research interests

  • GeoGebra and AI in learning

    Learning trajectories that pair GeoGebra applets with AI assistance to build understanding of statistical graphs and numeracy.

  • Rasch measurement

    Rating Scale and Many-Facet Rasch models for validating instruments, checking bias, and measuring change in reasoning.

  • Ethnomathematics

    Local food, folklore, and heritage sites as contexts for statistics and mathematics, through the Ethno-Flipped Classroom model.

  • Numeracy and inclusion

    Differentiated digital learning and neurodiversity-based inclusive mathematics education.
