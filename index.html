Mathematics Education · Medan — LinzPendidikan Matematika · Medan — Linz

Rahmi Ramadhani Umar

Associate Professor of Mathematics Education at Universitas Potensi Utama and JESH Postdoctoral Fellow of the Austrian Academy of Sciences at Johannes Kepler University Linz. She measures how students learn mathematics, and designs the cultural and digital contexts in which they learn it.

Associate Professor Pendidikan Matematika di Universitas Potensi Utama dan JESH Postdoctoral Fellow Akademi Ilmu Pengetahuan Austria di Johannes Kepler University Linz. Ia mengukur cara siswa belajar matematika, dan merancang konteks budaya serta digital tempat mereka mempelajarinya.

Chapter IBab IMeasurementPengukuran

Measuring what learners can do.Mengukur apa yang mampu dilakukan siswa.

A test score says little until items and learners are placed on the same scale. Her work uses Rasch models to build that scale: to validate questionnaires, to judge the quality and fairness of worksheets, and to see how reasoning changes over a course.

Skor tes belum banyak bercerita sampai butir soal dan siswa diletakkan pada skala yang sama. Risetnya memakai model Rasch untuk membangun skala itu: memvalidasi kuesioner, menilai mutu dan keadilan lembar kerja, serta melihat perubahan penalaran selama perkuliahan.

  1. Instruments that holdInstrumen yang kokoh

    Construct validity of a self-regulated learning questionnaire for senior high school students, checked item by item.

    Validitas konstruk kuesioner self-regulated learning untuk siswa SMA, diperiksa butir demi butir.

  2. Fairness across ratersAdil lintas penilai

    Many-Facet Rasch analysis of how experts rate numeracy worksheets and cloud classrooms, and where bias enters.

    Analisis Many-Facet Rasch tentang cara pakar menilai lembar kerja numerasi dan kelas daring, serta di mana bias masuk.

  3. Change you can seePerubahan yang terlihat

    Stacking and racking designs that show growth in informal statistical reasoning.

    Desain stacking dan racking yang memperlihatkan pertumbuhan penalaran statistik informal.

Chapter IIBab IICultureBudaya

Culture as a mathematical context.Budaya sebagai konteks matematis.

Temples, textiles, folklore and food already carry mathematics. She turns them into learning trajectories and worksheets, from the Nias culture of North Sumatra to the layout of Prambanan, and teaches them through the Ethno-Flipped Classroom model.

Candi, kain, cerita rakyat dan makanan sudah menyimpan matematika. Ia mengolahnya menjadi lintasan belajar dan lembar kerja, dari budaya Nias di Sumatera Utara sampai tata letak Prambanan, lalu mengajarkannya lewat model Ethno-Flipped Classroom.

  1. Sets in stoneHimpunan di batu candi

    Set theory read from the spatial layout of Prambanan temple.

    Teori himpunan yang dibaca dari tata ruang Candi Prambanan.

  2. Stories and functionsCerita dan fungsi

    Javanese folklore with moral values as a context for relations and functions.

    Cerita rakyat Jawa bernilai moral sebagai konteks relasi dan fungsi.

  3. Food and dataMakanan dan data

    Traditional food and technology in a learning trajectory for statistics.

    Makanan tradisional dan teknologi dalam lintasan belajar statistika.

Chapter IIIBab IIIToolsAlat

Tools that think alongside students.Alat yang ikut berpikir bersama siswa.

As a JESH fellow in Linz she studies how GeoGebra and AI assistance can work together in a learning trajectory, so that students build statistical graphs themselves and get feedback at each step.

Sebagai JESH fellow di Linz, ia meneliti bagaimana GeoGebra dan bantuan AI bisa bekerja bersama dalam satu lintasan belajar, sehingga siswa menyusun grafik statistik sendiri dan mendapat umpan balik di setiap langkah.

  1. GEASTRA

    GeoGebra-Supported AI for Statistical Graph Learning: five activities, each with AI guidance. Registered copyright, 2025.

    GeoGebra-Supported AI for Statistical Graph Learning: lima aktivitas, masing-masing dengan panduan AI. Hak cipta terdaftar, 2025.

  2. eSTEAM-AI

    A cross-subject STEAM project learning app integrated with AI. Registered copyright, 2025.

    Aplikasi pembelajaran projek STEAM lintas mata pelajaran yang terintegrasi AI. Hak cipta terdaftar, 2025.

  3. Deep learning trajectoriesLintasan belajar mendalam

    SOLO taxonomy and ethnomathematics combined in GeoGebra, published in Discover Education (2026).

    Taksonomi SOLO dan etnomatematika dipadukan dalam GeoGebra, terbit di Discover Education (2026).
